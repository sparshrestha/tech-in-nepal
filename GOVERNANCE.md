# Governance

Tech in Nepal is a community-curated directory. Its purpose is to make Nepal's technology ecosystem easier to discover without ranking, certifying or endorsing the entries.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose additions, corrections and removals through pull requests or listing requests.
- **Maintainers** review proposals, keep the catalog coherent and make merge decisions using the criteria below.

Maintainers may make small editorial changes for consistency and neutrality. Material changes should remain visible through a pull request or issue.

## Inclusion criteria

A listing should:

1. Have a clear and explainable connection to Nepal through its team, origin, operations, audience or subject matter.
2. Belong to an existing category, or justify why a new category would help readers find multiple relevant resources.
3. Use a stable, public URL that lets readers verify the listing.
4. Use a factual, concise and non-promotional description.
5. Add distinct value rather than duplicate an existing URL or entry.

Listings may be declined or removed when they are unrelated to the directory's scope, primarily spam or affiliate promotion, inaccessible for a sustained period, misleading, duplicated, or associated with credible safety or legal concerns. Removal is a catalog-maintenance decision, not a judgment about a person or organization.

## Review and decisions

Decisions are made in public repository discussions whenever practical. Maintainers consider the documented criteria, available evidence and long-term usefulness of the directory. There is no guaranteed review time or entitlement to inclusion.

No payment, sponsorship or personal relationship guarantees placement or prominence. Contributors and maintainers should disclose material conflicts of interest.

## Corrections, removals and appeals

Anyone may propose a correction or removal with a reason and, when relevant, a reliable source. If you disagree with a decision, open an issue with new evidence or clarification. Maintainers will explain the outcome against these criteria and may revisit earlier decisions when facts change.

## Changing this document

Changes to these rules should be proposed through a pull request so contributors can review the effect on future submissions.
